Game Title: Time Abyss

Game Description: Time Abyss is a platforming game about collecting and delivering rupees while avoiding enemies. The main mechanic I created for the game is a time travel mechanic that took inspiration from The Legend of Zelda Ocarina Of Time. When the player collects a powerup object they can then switch timelines and be in the same exact position they are in for each respective timeline.

How to Play: The player uses WASD or the arrow keys to move left and right, and forward and backward. The Q and E keys can be used to move the camera left and right as well. The player can press T if they have a powerup to switch between timelines at will. The goal is to collect all 10 of the green rupees that are split up between the timelines. They must then deliver them to the bucket in the present timeline. While also avoiding the eyeball enemies. The player can also press P to pause the game. The pause menu has a resume button and a main menu button. The map also features a fog particle effect that covers both the present and future timelines.

Game End: When the player runs out of time or falls off the map they get a game over screen and can go back to the main menu. If the player trys to deliver without enough coins they are spawned back at the beginning and are shown a warning text. If the player delivers the coins they are shown an End screen and can go back to the main menu.
